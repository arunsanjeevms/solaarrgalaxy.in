Solar FAQ

Solar in Karur: your questions answered

Straight answers on cost, subsidy, installation and savings from Karur's solar team.

Which is the best solar company in Karur?

Look for a local company with a long track record, trusted brands and service nearby. Solaarr Galaxy has served Karur since 2008, has completed 1000+ installations across Tamil Nadu, installs brands like Loom Solar, UTL, V-Guard, Microtek and Okaya, and handles design, installation, subsidy paperwork and maintenance with its own team.

How much Government subsidy can I get for rooftop solar?

For a 3 kW on-grid rooftop solar system, the Government subsidy is up to ₹1,00,000. It is credited about 45 days after the plant is completed, and your EB sanctioned load must be 3.3 kW. We help you with the subsidy paperwork.

What does a 3 kW rooftop solar system cost in Karur?

Our complete 3 kW on-grid package costs ₹2,10,000 including tax. After the ₹1,00,000 Government subsidy, you effectively pay about ₹1,10,000. The final price depends on your site and your choice of brand.

How many units will a 3 kW solar system generate?

About 360 units a month, based on roughly 4 units per kW per day. Actual output depends on your roof direction and shading.

How much roof space do I need for solar panels?

Plan for about 100 sq.ft of shade-free roof per kW, so a 3 kW system needs around 300 sq.ft. We check this during the free site visit.

How long does solar installation take?

About 3 weeks from a clear purchase order. That covers supply, mounting, wiring, earthing, testing, commissioning and net-meter hook-up.

What warranty comes with the solar system?

Solar panels carry a 15-year product warranty plus a 10-year performance warranty, the inverter has a 10-year warranty, and the balance of system is covered for 1 year from commissioning.

What other products does Solaarr Galaxy install?

Besides rooftop solar power plants, we supply and install solar water heaters, solar street and home lighting, CCTV cameras, UPS and batteries, RO water purifiers, intercoms and video door phones, and biometric attendance machines in Karur and across Tamil Nadu.
