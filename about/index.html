About us

About Solaarr Galaxy, Karur’s solar company since 2008

Since 2008 we have supplied, installed and maintained rooftop solar and solar products for homes, businesses and institutions across Tamil Nadu.

Our story

Karur's local solar partner for energy independence

India's energy sector is changing at an unprecedented pace. Since 2008, Solaarr Galaxy, also known as Solar Galaxy in Karur, has helped homes, businesses and institutions across Tamil Nadu look beyond cost savings toward long-term sustainability.

As a full-service company with complete in-house capabilities, we guide clients through evaluation, design, planning, procurement, construction, operation, maintenance and financing. Over 1000 clients trust us, and we have completed more than 1000 installations across the state.

"We don't want to push our ideas on to customers. We simply want to make what they want."

Our Mission
